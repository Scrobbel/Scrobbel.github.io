CITOV

  • Surface:Terrestrial
  • Size:Dwarf Sized (Light Gravity)
  • Atmosphere:Terraformed
  • Climate:Arid
  • Population:Millions
  • Controlled By:Corporation (Seer Makrosystem)
Citov was chosen to keep corporate officers in comfort. The shipyards crowding its skies have warped the trade lanes around it, guaranteeing the continued success of the planet. The planet is strained by the population. Rent costs force many workers to live in orbit—a deliberate measure to ensure property values planetside stay high.
