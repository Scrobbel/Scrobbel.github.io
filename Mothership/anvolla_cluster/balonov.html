BALONOV

  • Surface:Terrestrial
  • Size:Earth-Sized Planet
  • Atmosphere:Terraformed
  • Climate:Arid
  • Population:Millions
  • Controlled By:Corporation (Seer Makrosystem)
Balonov was a mining colony. The primary resources have been extracted, leaving a decaying post-industrial ruin stricken with extreme poverty. Much of the population lives in the sprawling industrial ruins of the Downport City (Markhamn 01).
