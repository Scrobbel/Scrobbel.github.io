ANDROID BACKGROUNDS
Androids have been revised into unique set of Backgrounds that recontextualize their existence and role in the Mothership world. They are a standard robotic assembly that functions differently from humans.
They operate on different rules. Androids:
  • Don’t need to breathe, sleep, eat, or drink.
  • Have [-] on all rolls if not charged for 1d5+5 hours after 3 days before going unconscious in 1d5 days.
  • Can recover 1d10 Health during Short Term Recovery by making an Intellect check for repairs.
  • Are immune to radiation.
  • Make a Body Save when taking electrical damage or is Stunned for 1 round.
  • TR:Close non-Android allies make Fear Saves at [-]
