PLAYER IMPROVEMENTS

PERKS
Perks are special, powerful abilities player characters can learn in the form of Perk Cards. They can only be learned during Shore Leave by:
  • The Warden awarding them.
  • Converting 8 points of Stress.
Regardless, no more than 2 cards can be learned each Shore Leave.

Whenever a player would gain a Wound, they can instead choose to discard a Perk Card. A discarded talent is gone until learned again.
Regarding requirements:
  • HS refers to your High Score.
  • Once you’ve gained a Perk Card you no longer need to meet its requirements, unless you re-learn it.
