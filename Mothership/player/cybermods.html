CYBERMODS

INTRO
There are two kinds of CYBERMODS (MODS): CYBERWARE and SLICKWARE:
Cyberware is cybernetic hardware that is physically installed and augments your body.
Slickware is software you install in your brain via a Slicksocket.

Every player has a certain number of SLOTS they can use to install mods. The tens-digit of your Strength is how many Cyberware SLOTS you have, while the tens-digit of your Intellect is how many Slickware SLOTS you have, provided a Slicksocket is installed.
DANGERS OF ROGUE INTELLIGENCE
Almost all Cybermods are fitted with their own Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help facilitate connection between host and device. Normally, a host need not worry about what these AIs get up to, and they are far from the level of sentience an Android would be. Despite this, the dangers of an AI going rogue always exists, see Mutations.
INSTALLING CYBERMODS
At a Chop Shop or Slickbay you can install Cyberware and Slickware, respectively. Outside of this, expect disadvantage on rolls, unless a professional is doing it.

Cyberware: Make a Body Save at [+]. Failure means its installed, but you gain a Malfunction. Critical Failure also gives a Mutation and you take Xd10 damage, where X is the amount of SLOTS being filled.

Slickware: Make a Sanity Save at [+]. Failure means its installed, but you gain a Malfunction. Critical Failure also gives a Mutation and you lose 1d10+1 Intellect.

MALFUNCTIONS AND MUTATIONS
Malfunctions can be removed at a cost equal to 100 times the result rolled when gaining it.
Mutations occur when an AI does not properly integrate with the host. These are all edge-cases and have no set removal cost, but they usually require someone extremely skilled.
