PSIONICS

Those with supernatural abilities unexplainable by science, manifested through sheer will - and with great risk - are called Psions.
Psionic Cards can only be learned by a character with the Latent Psionics Perk Card, and cannot be discarded. Whenever you would learn a Perk Card, you can instead learn a Psionic Card.
Using Psionics
All Psionic Cards belong to a Discipline - either Body or Sanity - and are categorized into three tiers:
  • Simple (+30) ●
  • Regular (+20) ●●
  • Complex (+10) ●●●
Using Psionics requires a Psionic Check, which is a Save depending on the Discipline, modified by its tier. Regardless of the outcome, you gain the Stress equal to its tier.
Failure
If you fail a Psionic Check you can choose to succeed and immediately roll a Psionic Panic Check. Critical fails always force a Psionic Panic Check, and can not be turned into successes.
Duration
Psionic Card durations are categorized as:
  • Focus.As long as the Psion focuses on it. Skills nor Psionics can be used, and only simple tasks such as walking, talking or resting is possible. If concentration would be lost, a Psionic Check is made to maintain it.
  • Instant.Immediate, no lasting effects.
  • Minutes.1d10x10 minutes.
  • Hours.2d10 hours.
  • Days.1d5 days.

Mind Scatter●●●
SANITY - CLOSE - INSTANT
Mix, meld and mash all non-Psychic minds in range together. Each target takes Xd10 (AA) damage, where X is the amount of targets. You then suffer 1d10 Stress and must roll a Psionic Panic Check. Special Wound: Condition: Mind melded. Your emotions, memories and secrets have been mixed with others.
Probe Mind●●
SANITY - ADJACENT - FOCUS
Delve into a mind and read its emotions, memories and secrets. Works on animal and alien minds, but may be… difficult to comprehend.
