PLAYER IMPROVEMENTS

This document is in english, as it concerns the rules of the game itself.
Following are options offered for players as they get stronger after surviving horrific adventures.
PERK CARDS. Unique abilities offered during Shore Leave.
CYBERMODS. Electronic modifications for your body and soul.
PSIONICS. Dangerous supernatural abilities born from the mind.
MAKING A CHARACTER
  1. CLASS.Pick your class, choosing from Teamster, Marine or Scientist.
  2. ROLL AND ADJUST.Roll all your stats, and then adjust it according to your class.
  3. SKILLS.Select your skills, according to your class.
  4. BACKGROUND.Choose your Background. Write down its Trauma Response (if any) and any other additional effects, such as swapping skills. Pick one of the stats your background buffed and write it down under your Skills. This is the stat you are improving.
    • If you picked an Android background, note down the special rules for Androids.
  5. ITEMS AND CREDITS.Finally, roll your items and credits.
